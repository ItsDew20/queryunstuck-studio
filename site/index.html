Short animated explainers on SQL, data engineering, data analysis, AI/ML and data system design, made to help you understand concepts and prepare for interviews.

About the QueryUnstuck Publisher app

QueryUnstuck Publisher is the internal tool we use to publish our own videos to our own Instagram and YouTube accounts and to read the performance metrics of those posts. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account. See the privacy policy for details.
